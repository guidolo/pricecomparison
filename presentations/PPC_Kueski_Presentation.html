Mitigating Data & Freshness Risks

Challenge: Heterogeneous Catalogs

Combining diverse, low-quality merchant data is costly.

Mitigation: AI-Assisted Mapping

Leverage AI to homologate variants, deduplicate listings, and map disparate product attributes to a single, unified common schema.

Challenge: Stale Pricing Data

Real-time synchronization across the entire catalog is out of scope.

Mitigation: Intra-Day Refresh

Implement prioritized, ad-hoc intra-day syncs specifically for comparator-listed items and high-velocity SKUs to ensure accuracy.

Path to POC: Balancing UX with Technical Feasibility

Option A: Native PPC with Price Aggregation Layer
Build a native app layer consuming normalized data from multiple merchants.

Pros

Full UX control; Highly scalable.

Cons

Requires heavy investment in catalog normalization and aggregation architecture.

Option B: Catalog Enrichment (Lighter Approach)
Enhance the current catalog view with basic attributes and per-merchant pricing.

Pros

Faster time-to-market; Lower development risk.

Cons

Caps the potential for maximum conversion improvement.
